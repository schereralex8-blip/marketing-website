Dining

Three spots. Zero bad meals.

From a classy steakhouse to a wing-loaded sports lounge to a laid-back outdoor grill.

Restaurant 1

Millar Munches

Vibe: Classy coastal steakhouse

A super classy Peter Millar themed restaurant serving top-tier steakhouse meals. Perfect for going out with family or friends for a high-end dinner after golf.

Featured Foods

  • Prime ribeye
  • Filet mignon
  • Loaded baked potatoes
  • Wedge salad
Restaurant 2

Whale Wings

Vibe: Fun sports lounge with preppy coastal vibes

A Vineyard Vines themed sports bar and lounge packed with flat screen TVs everywhere. Perfect spot to eat chicken wings, watch the games, and hang out.

Featured Foods

  • Boneless & bone-in wings
  • Buffalo
  • BBQ
  • Honey garlic
  • Fries
  • Sliders
Restaurant 3

Frat Fried

Vibe: Laid-back, casual summer hangout spot

A chill frat-themed outdoor grill house serving up all your favorite comfort food straight off the grill.

Featured Foods

  • Fried chicken baskets
  • Grilled burgers
  • Hot dogs
  • Fries
  • Milkshakes
